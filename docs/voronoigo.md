## Voronoi Go

I worked on ContinuGo on and off between start of 2023 and May 2024, then lost interest. My interest was resurged in 2026 upon seeing (and playing!) [Voronoi Go](https://voronoigo.com/).

Cameron got a lot of things right with Voronoi Go:

- The rules are simple and elegant
- The scoring makes sense and follows directly from the rules (I stole the voronoi scoring already)
- The UI is great, deadzones (I stole those immediately)
- There's a backend, with user accounts and chat and a reasonably strong bot
- Last not least there's a Discord server where people talk

I believe it does one-and-a-half things wrong:

- **There are no mutual cuts**: if you're not connected, the other guy is.
- The half a thing wrong is the weird super-far slides that allow one to steal all the territory near the border.
